## Tender Ruins

I photographed the afterlife of things: wrappers, rust, plastic, paper—objects that outlive their usefulness but not their presence. Beauty, stubborn and accidental. This album collects textures, shapes, and small surprises from the world’s unofficial museums—streets, bins, and backrooms.
