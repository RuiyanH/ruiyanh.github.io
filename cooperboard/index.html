What happens under the hood

From ambient work to one defensible intervention.

CooperBoard separates observed activity, inferred coordination state, policy, and delivery instead of asking one model to do everything.

  1. 01
    ⌁

    Observe locally

    The macOS app turns periodic screenshots into structured activity summaries. Raw images remain on the participant’s Mac.

  2. 02
    ◎

    Resolve context

    Slack events, project state, tasks, and derived activity are joined inside the authorized project boundary.

  3. 03
    ⌇

    Detect carefully

    Rule and model-backed detectors produce evidence-linked candidates with explicit provenance and confidence.

  4. 04
    ↗

    Act with limits

    Policy decides whether to hold, ask, remind, or deliver. Supported actions are scoped, idempotent, and audited.

System design

Privacy is an architectural boundary, not a promise in the footer.

CooperBoard keeps raw visual data at the edge and centralizes only structured, study-scoped signals needed for coordination.
